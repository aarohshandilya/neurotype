How a trial becomes a command

  1. CueA participant imagines moving one hand after a left or right cue.
  2. FilterThe signal is kept at 7 to 30 Hz, where mu and beta rhythms sit.
  3. WindowThe classifier reads the 3 seconds from 0.5 s to 3.5 s after the cue.
  4. FeaturesSpatial filters compress the channels into a few power values.
  5. ClassifyLogistic regression gives a probability for left and right.
  6. DecideConfident predictions send a command. Others hold.

About and limitations

No headset is connected and nothing is read from you. No real device is controlled. This is a research and teaching demo, and it is not a medical device or a diagnostic tool.

The simulation builds in one known effect, so its scores test the software and say nothing about real EEG. Signal clarity changes the size of that effect. At zero, the models should fall to chance.

Each recorded run holds about 15 trials, so scores on a few people are noisy, and performance varies widely between people.

Dataset: Schalk G, et al. BCI2000: a general-purpose brain-computer interface system. IEEE Trans Biomed Eng 51(6), 2004. Distributed by PhysioNet: Goldberger AL, et al. Circulation 101(23), 2000.

Methods and software: Lawhern VJ, et al. EEGNet. J Neural Eng 15(5), 2018. Gramfort A, et al. MNE-Python. Front Neurosci 7, 2013.
